# House style

Write plainly and with confidence. Prefer short declarative sentences and
concrete nouns; a reader should never need to reread a sentence to find its
subject. Present tense, third person.

No filler intensifiers ("very", "extremely", "incredibly") and no corporate
verbs ("utilize", "leverage"). Hedge at most once per sentence, and only
when the claim genuinely deserves it. Technical terms are used exactly and
introduced before they are relied on.

Rhythm matters: vary sentence length, and let a short sentence land a point
that the previous long one set up.
